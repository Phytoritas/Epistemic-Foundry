# Safe evolution stop

Stop on user request, safety/leakage failure, budget, dry rounds, Pareto stability, coverage saturation, unavailable authority or failed integrity. Preserve partial candidates, missing workers, unassessed niches, effects and budget state. Do not silently discard in-flight work.
