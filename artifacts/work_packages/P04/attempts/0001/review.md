# P04-0001 primary-session separate adversarial review

- Reviewer: primary session in a separate adversarial pass under the
  product-owner instruction forbidding subagents and Fleet;
  actor_independence=false is recorded, not hidden.
- Both vocabularies are read from their declaring schemas, so a
  verdict or recommendation this component invented would not resolve.
- A majority cannot promote. The recommendation is bounded above by
  the deterministic P02 ceiling; four briefs unanimously saying
  ENTAILED still cannot lift it. The majority is computed and recorded
  so a reader can see it existed, and the sealed gate carries an
  explicit statement that it did not influence the recommendation,
  which a rehashed edit cannot flip.
- A sustained method veto or any unanswered cross-examination forces
  BLOCK, because an objection nobody answered is not a detail to be
  weighed against a majority. Every open challenge must also appear as
  an unresolved issue in the adjudication, and the strongest dissent
  must be cited by the adjudication that decided against it.
- An attempt to override a deterministic gate is refused rather than
  absorbed, and a sealed gate may not record a successful one.
- The attestor reads structure, not persuasion. The pack carries ids,
  hashes, statuses, ceilings and counts; every persuasive field name
  is refused anywhere in the pack, including nested inside the
  attestation's own checks, so rhetoric cannot re-enter through the
  reviewer's worksheet. The judge's rationale never reaches the pack.
- Independence is checked, not asserted. The attestor may not be the
  judge, must state its independence, must name the adjudication its
  pack describes, and must rest on exactly that pack: extra inputs are
  refused as readily as wrong ones. Its checks must match the
  canonical check shape, so an attestation cannot claim scrutiny it
  did not record. A failing attestation still seals and says so.
- Residual limitations: the judge's reasoning arrives as an input
  adjudication rather than being produced here; the component cannot
  verify that the attestor actually ran the checks it lists or
  cryptographically verify its signature; and the promotion commit
  belongs to the governance gates. This review is not external
  actor-independent certification.
