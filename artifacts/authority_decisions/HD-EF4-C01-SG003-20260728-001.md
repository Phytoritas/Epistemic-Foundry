## Canonical PromotionDecision semantics

The ordered levels are `INBOX < CANDIDATE < LITERATURE_GROUNDED <
VALIDATION_SCREENED < EMPIRICALLY_TESTED < REPLICATED`.

`PROMOTE` requires a non-null `granted_level` equal to `requested_level`.
`CONDITIONAL` requires a non-null level strictly above the candidate's current
level and strictly below the requested level. `REJECT`, `UNDERDETERMINED`, and
`BLOCKED` require `granted_level: null` and must not mutate the candidate's
existing level. Demotion or invalidation is a separate reassessment workflow.
The legacy strings `PILOT` and `HYPOTHESIS_PASSPORT_ONLY` have no general
automatic mapping and remain forbidden on new write paths.
